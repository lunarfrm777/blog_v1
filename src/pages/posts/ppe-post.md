Lately I have been thinking lots about whats it actually takes to strengthen skills,, or learn something new. It can be daunting,, what if i fail? what if it isnt the way I intended it to be from the beginning? ((does it actually ever end up being that way?))

Failing to start,, which leads to starting to fail. what do i mean?

When we want to start something new,, something we have never tried before,, we will have no experience with whatever it is we are beginning -  we have the skills and experience we have gained over life until that moment,, which gives us some courage to even venture into a new path,, but still what waits are the new patterns to be made,, patience in trying and failing,, trying again,, succeeding and repeating,, which then ultimately leads to experience!! 

Experience always feels like "that thing" that takes away the fear of messing up because we know we have done it before,, and have been able to succeed. 

It made me wonder,, who are we failing for?

Ourselves of course,, but it can usually not be that crystal clear from the beginning. It can be a whole range of emotions of why we shouldn't move forward,, why it won't work out,, why we shouldn't try even before attempting to take one step.

I WANT TO SAY!!!! TRY ANYWAY!!!!! 

Take one step!! Fail!! Take a step back for a moment!! And try again!!!!

Over time it seems that the more we try to fail,, rather than failing to try,, the confidence is gained to try again and again,, in different avenues,, different pathways and journeys in life!!
Until maybe one day or never,, the fear goes away when we are restarting. We have the prior knowledge of experience to a time where we tried something for the first time,, tried while scared,, and was still able to make it through to learn something new and unexpected that we never thought was possible before!!!

Now go try and fail!!! Try again!!! You got this <3
